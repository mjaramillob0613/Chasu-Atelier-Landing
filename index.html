Our Story

We are a company of women passionate about handmade arts. In a world full of technology and stress, we have found a greater purpose in helping people build community and creative spaces to give their minds an active pause.

We work with all the love in the world and welcome every suggestion to improve day by day. Because we believe that every stitch is a moment of calm, connection, and joy.
